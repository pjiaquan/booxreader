Original Text
"Affordances provide strong clues to the operations of things. Plates are for pushing. Knobs are for turning. Slots are for inserting things into."
AI Response
Norman's concept of affordances describes the relationship between an object's properties and a user's capabilities — specifically, what actions become apparent based on how something looks or feels.
When you see a door handle, you intuitively know to pull; a flat plate suggests pushing. This is a perceived affordance — the design communicates its purpose without instructions.
Good design makes affordances visible, reducing the need for labels or manuals.
